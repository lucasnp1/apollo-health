FAQ

Know before you log.

Apollo is built for people who take their protocol seriously and want the numbers in one place, without handing their health data to anyone.

01What does Apollo track?+

Injections (compound, dose, site, vial), blood pressure and pulse, weight and body measurements, symptom check-ins, and lab results with their reference ranges. Everything lands on one timeline so you can read the bloods against the doses.

02Is my data private?+

Yes. Apollo is local-first: your data lives on your phone and syncs to your own account over HTTPS. There are no trackers, no analytics and no third-party SDKs. Lab reports are read on the device. You can export everything or delete the whole account from Settings at any time.

03Which lab reports work?+

Any PDF with a text layer, and scanned PDFs or photos too, which Apollo reads with on-device OCR. It knows the naming used by UK, US and Brazilian labs, handles mmol/L and mg/dL, decimal commas, and values like "<0.3". You review every row before it is saved.

04Is this medical advice?+

No. Apollo is a tracking tool with a smart second opinion built in. It flags what is out of a TRT-aware range and explains what usually matters, but every decision about your protocol belongs to you and a licensed clinician.

05Does it work on iPhone and Android?+

Yes. Apollo is a web app you add to your home screen, so it opens like a native app on both, with no store required. Onboarding walks you through the two taps it takes.

06Does it work offline?+

Yes. Logging, the timeline and your bloods all work without signal. Everything syncs the next time you are online.

07Can I share it with my doctor?+

Pick a date range and what to include, then export a clean PDF or a CSV. Doses, blood pressure, weight, symptoms and every lab marker, in a format a clinic can actually read.

08What if I forget my password?+

When you sign up you get eight one-time recovery codes. Any one of them lets you set a new password. Lost those too? Email us from your account address and we will send you a one-time link.

09Can I cancel or delete my account?+

Any time. Cancel Pro and you keep it until the end of what you paid for. Delete the account from Settings and everything is wiped from our servers straight away, along with the copy on your phone.

10What is free, exactly?+

All the logging: injections, blood pressure, weight, symptoms, manual lab entry, timeline, sync, CSV export. Pro adds lab PDF and photo import, smart analysis with trends, and the doctor-ready PDF.
