Estradiol is the estrogen men make from testosterone. On TRT it rises with the dose, and that is mostly good: it protects bone, joints, HDL, mood and libido. The mistake most people make in year one is treating the estradiol number like a problem to fix.

## Read the ratio, not the number

Take testosterone in ng/dL and divide by estradiol in pg/mL. Around 15 to 30 is where most men feel best. A testosterone of 900 with an estradiol of 45 is a ratio of 20: fine, even though 45 is above some lab ranges. A testosterone of 400 with an estradiol of 45 is a ratio of 9: that is a lot of aromatising for the dose.

Ask for the sensitive (LC-MS/MS) estradiol assay. The standard immunoassay reads men's levels poorly and produces false highs.

## What high and low feel like

- **High:** water retention, puffy face, sore or puffy nipples, moodiness, emotional over-reaction.
- **Crashed (too low):** dry, aching joints, flat mood, no libido, poor erections, fatigue, sometimes anxiety.

Crashed feels worse than high, lasts longer, and is almost always caused by an aromatase inhibitor.

## What people try, in order

1. **Split the dose.** Big infrequent shots aromatise at the peak. The same weekly dose in two or three injections often brings estradiol down and the ratio into place with no other change.
2. **Body fat.** Aromatase lives in fat. Leaner men run lower estradiol on the same dose. This is slow but real.
3. **Alcohol.** It raises estradiol for days. Skip it before a draw and notice whether the symptoms track your weekends.
4. **Soft options.** DIM and calcium-D-glucarate are what people reach for before a drug. Evidence is thin; some feel a difference.
5. **An aromatase inhibitor, last.** A quarter of a tablet, once or twice a week, only for clear symptoms that the steps above did not fix. Retest in three to four weeks. If joints ache and libido drops, that is crashed estradiol: stop.

## When the number is low without an AI

Very low body fat, a hard diet, or a low dose. Estradiol follows testosterone; a low dose with a low estradiol usually means the dose is low, not that the estradiol needs raising.

## Keep it on one line

Estradiol swings with timing and assay more than most markers. Same lab, same draw timing, and a line showing every result next to the testosterone it came with is the only way to know whether a change did anything.
